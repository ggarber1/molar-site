Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Molar ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our dental practice management platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by dental practices and their patients, including names, contact information, appointment details, and clinical records necessary to operate the platform.

SMS Communications

Dental practices that use Molar send text messages to their own patients through our platform. These terms describe how patients opt in, what they receive, and how to stop.

How consent is obtained: Patients opt in when they give their mobile number to their dental practice and agree to receive texts — either by checking the SMS consent box (“I consent to receiving appointment reminders and practice communications via SMS”) on the practice’s digital intake form, or by telling the practice’s staff, who record the patient’s preference in their patient record. Patients who decline, or who later opt out, are marked as opted out and receive no text messages.

Types of messages sent:

  • Appointment reminders (typically 48 hours and 24 hours before a visit)
  • Confirmation requests — reply YES to confirm, or NO if you can’t make it
  • Links to complete the practice’s new-patient intake form

Message frequency: Usually 2–3 messages per scheduled appointment.

Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation and no further text messages. To start receiving texts again, contact your dental practice.

Help: For help, contact your dental practice or email us at g.s.garber@outlook.com.

No sharing for marketing: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect patient and practice data. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell patient data to third parties.

HIPAA

Molar operates as a Business Associate under HIPAA. All protected health information (PHI) is handled in accordance with applicable federal and state privacy regulations.

Contact

For questions about this policy or our data practices, contact us at g.s.garber@outlook.com.
